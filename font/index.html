Questions

Is it a real font or just a picture?

A real font file. You install it like any other font and type with it. Even the quick font from this page is a real OTF.

Will it work in Word, Canva and Figma?

Yes. OTF and TTF install on Windows and Mac and then show up in Word, Figma and most apps. Canva lets you upload your own fonts on its paid plans.

My handwriting is messy. Is that a problem?

No, that is the point. We keep the character of your hand and only fix what breaks a font, like letters that touch the box, sit off the line or are too faint.

Can I get Thai letters?

Not in the standard sheet yet. Message us first and we will tell you if it can be done for your text.
