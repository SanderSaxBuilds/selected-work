One graphic. Three ways to interact.

A flat process graphic becomes a layered object. Scroll to separate the layers, hover to change the viewing angle, or use the controls below.

Inspect the interaction

The source remains editable. The three surfaces are drawn as SVG, with depth, shading and movement supplied by CSS transforms.

Compact0%Exploded

Compact view. Scroll or drag the slider to separate the layers.

Scroll

Page progress maps to layer depth. The slider also works without scrolling.

Hover / pointer

The viewing angle follows the pointer within a bounded range, then returns to its base angle.

Click / keyboard

Buttons and the slider work with a keyboard. Motion can be paused, and reduced-motion preferences are respected.
