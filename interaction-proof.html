CSS and SVG interaction study

Four plates, one poster.

A printed poster is four thin layers of ink: cyan, magenta, yellow and black. Scroll to pull the plates apart, tilt the stack with the pointer, switch plates off, and knock the registration out to see the colour fringes a press operator hunts for.

CombinedIn register
01 / Separate

Pull the plates apart

Keep scrolling. Your position here sets how far apart the four plates float, from one printed sheet to a stack of films.

Your device asks for reduced motion, so scrolling leaves the plates still. The slider below does the same job.

02 / Tilt

Look along the stack

Move the pointer across the poster. The angle follows within a small range and settles back when you leave. Each film carries only its own ink, so the red sun is magenta on one plate and yellow on another.

03 / Plates

Switch inks off

Take a plate away and see what it was doing. Without yellow the sun turns pink, without cyan the sea goes purple and the sky disappears.

04 / Registration

Knock it out of register

On press, each plate has to land on the same spot. The plates settle back into one sheet here. Drag the slider and the cyan and magenta plates drift, which shows up first as coloured edges on the sun and at the crosshair marks in the corners.

Measured as if the poster were printed on an A3 sheet.

Four SVG files and one variable

Each plate is a small SVG drawn in a single ink. Flat, they sit on a white sheet with mix-blend-mode: multiply, which is how inks mix on paper. Separated, the same drawings move onto films in a 3D stack, spaced by a CSS custom property fed into translateZ.

Scroll updates run once per animation frame. Touching a slider or button hands control to you until you scroll again. No canvas, no libraries.

function setDepth(raw) {
  const n = clamp(Math.round(raw), 0, 100);
  plates.forEach((p, i) =>
    p.style.setProperty('--z', (i - 1.5) * (4 + n * 1.1) + 'px'));
  composite.style.opacity = Math.max(0, 1 - n / 12);
  assembly.style.opacity = Math.min(1, n / 8);
}
