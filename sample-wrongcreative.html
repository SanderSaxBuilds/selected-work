Three moments worth practicing

Each module takes one moment, shows what a clearer version sounds like and ends with a short exercise you can try on your next tour. Here is one example tour, line by line. Rewrite each agent line to hear the difference.

An example tour, written for the course. One prospect, two homes that fit.

0 of 3 rewritten
i.Open with focused questions
Prospect
“Hi, I'm here for the two o'clock tour.”
Agent
“Great. So, what are you looking for?”“Welcome. What has to be true about the next place for you to say yes? And when do you need to move by?”

As often saidA broad opener. The prospect has to guess what you want to know, so you start the tour without a plan.As practicedTwo focused questions. The answers decide which two homes you show and what you say about them.

ii.Explain the options that matter
Prospect
“Quiet matters most, I work nights. And I have a car.”
Agent
“Sure. Let me show you the gym, the pool, the rooftop and the lounge, and then we'll see the units.”“Then two homes fit. 4B faces the courtyard and comes with a covered space. 7A is larger but faces the street. The parking fee for 4B is one thing I still need to confirm.”

As often saidEvery amenity in order. Nothing links back to what the prospect just said.As practicedTwo options, compared on the prospect's own priorities, with a known fact kept apart from what still needs checking.

iii.Agree a follow-up they can act on
Prospect
“I like them both. I'll need to talk it over with my partner.”
Agent
“Of course. I'll send you some details.”“Of course. I'll check the parking fee for 4B and text you by noon tomorrow. Would Saturday at ten work for your partner to see it?”

As often saidA promise with no what and no when. The next step is left open.As practicedThe open question, who acts on it, by when, and a time for the next visit.
