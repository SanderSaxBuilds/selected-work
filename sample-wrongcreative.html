Room 1, front doorAsk two clear questions
Prospect
“Hi, I'm here for the two o'clock tour.”
Agent
“Great. So, what are you looking for?”“Welcome. What has to be true about the next place for you to say yes? And when do you need to move by?”

BeforeA broad question. Nothing to decide which homes to show.AfterTwo questions that decide which homes to show.

Room 2, living roomExplain the options
Prospect
“Quiet matters most, I work nights. And I have a car.”
Agent
“Sure. Let me show you the gym, the pool, the rooftop and the lounge, and then we'll see the units.”“Then two homes fit. 4B faces the courtyard and comes with a covered space. 7A is larger but faces the street. The parking fee for 4B is one thing I still need to confirm.”

BeforeEvery amenity, none of it tied to what they said.AfterOnly what matters to them, and what still needs checking.

Room 3, kitchen tableAgree the next step
Prospect
“I like them both. I'll need to talk it over with my partner.”
Agent
“Of course. I'll send you some details.”“Of course. I'll check the parking fee for 4B and text you by noon tomorrow. Would Saturday at ten work for your partner to see it?”

BeforeNo task and no time.AfterWho does what, by when, and the next visit.
