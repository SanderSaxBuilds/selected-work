Three moments worth practicing

Each module takes one moment, shows what a clearer version sounds like and ends with a short exercise you can try on your next tour.

1

Open with focused questions

Swap a broad opener for two questions that find out what the prospect needs to understand first.

Instead of“So, what are you looking for?”

2

Explain the options that matter

Compare two options using the priorities the prospect already gave you, and separate facts from things to confirm.

Instead ofA full tour of every amenity

3

Agree a follow-up they can act on

Summarize the open question, name the next step and who owns it, and set a time.

Instead of“I'll send you some details.”
