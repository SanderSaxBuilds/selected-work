How a team session runs

Each session takes about 45 minutes and covers one module. The discussion guide gives the lead the questions and the timings.

  1. Watch the lessons

    Each person takes the module's two lessons on their own before the session.

  2. Do the exercise alone

    Everyone writes an answer to the practice exercise without seeing anyone else's.

  3. Compare as a team

    Read the answers side by side and talk through what changed and why.

  4. Pick one thing to try

    Record one change on the worksheet and check back on it at the next session.

Questions from team leads

Who runs the sessions?

Anyone on the team can. The discussion guide has the questions, the timings and what to record on the worksheet.

Can we add learners later?

Yes. Seats are shared across the team for the twelve months of access, up to ten at any time.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. The sessions work on a video call. Share the worksheet on screen and fill it in together.
