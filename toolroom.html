Questions

Good to know.

Can I buy these tools here?

No. This storefront runs a demo checkout that saves a test order in your browser. No payment is taken, no license is issued and no email is sent.

What do I download?

Each tool has a small sample file, either CSV or plain text, that shows the kind of output it produces. Full product archives are not served from this page.

Where does my library live?

In this browser on this device, when browser storage is available. Backup creates a portable JSON file, and restoring checks the file before anything changes. Private windows and cleared site data remove the local library.

Is this connected to Etsy or another shop?

No. Nothing here connects to Etsy, Payhip or a payment provider. A live store would need its own checkout and delivery service.
