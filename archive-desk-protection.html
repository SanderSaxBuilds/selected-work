What protection covers

Inside the lockFile names, contents and the manifest are encrypted. The outside filename and file size stay visible. This is not a password-protected ZIP, so open .adlock files with Archive Desk.

On your deviceEverything happens in this tab, with no upload, account or sync. Passphrase fields clear after each attempt. A lost passphrase cannot be recovered, and a compromised device can expose files while you use them.

How it is builtAES-256-GCM with a fresh random salt and nonce, and PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 at 600,000 iterations. It has not had an independent security audit, so keep another tested copy of important work.
