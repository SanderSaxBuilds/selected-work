Mobile interface concept

Review expenses without losing the next step.

Clearline gives a small team one place to see the month, find what needs a decision and approve or send back an expense in two taps.

  • Spending against budget, recalculated as decisions change
  • Filter by status and search by merchant or teammate
  • Approve or request a correction, with one-step undo
  • Works with a keyboard, and Escape steps back from a detail

Speculative sample for a public brief that asked for a refresh of an existing expense app. It is not the buyer's app and was not commissioned.

Clearline, the team and every expense are fictional. Decisions stay on this page and nothing is sent anywhere.
