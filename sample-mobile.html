          What the team receives

          Nothing sent yet. Each decision you make in the app lands here as the note the teammate gets, worded for them, not for the finance team. This is what they look like:

          • OP
            Example, to Owen Park

            Example, to Priya Nair

            Foundry Type, $49.00, came back to you. Itemized receipt missing.

            Checked on every receipt

            • Same merchant, same amount. A likely double claim is flagged before anyone pays it twice.
            • Budget after approval. The card shows where the category lands against its monthly limit.
            • Receipt on file. Approving without one asks for a short note for the auditor.
            • Undo for every decision. A mistaken swipe is one tap, or the Z key, away from fixed.

            About this sample

            A speculative sample for a public brief that asked for a refresh of an existing expense app. It is not the buyer's app and was not commissioned.

            Clearline, the team and every receipt are fictional. Totals, flags and budget figures are worked out from the sample receipts. Decisions stay on this page and nothing is sent anywhere.
