Keep the finish new.

  1. Wash by hand. Warm water, mild soap and a soft bottle brush.
  2. Skip the scourer. Abrasive pads polish the grain flat and leave a shine.
  3. Dry with the lid off. The seal lasts longer when it is not stored damp.
