One daily rate covers teaching, room and board.

Each study day has Rasidaton Thai yoga at 07.00, six hours of teaching and supervised practice from 09.00 to 16.00, and a herbal steam bath at 17.30. Pool, bicycles and WiFi are included.

A live-in school

Almost every student lives here, in a private room in one of the old wooden farmhouses, and everyone eats together at the big table.

For the hottest weeks there is a new training sala that can be air conditioned, with space for 16 and a strict class limit of 14. Partners, guests and even children are welcome.

Homprang Chaleekanha

Licensed by the Thai Ministry of Public Health as a traditional doctor, herbal pharmacologist, traditional midwife and massage teacher. She grew up in a village on the Burmese border, where her grandmother was the local midwife and herbalist.

Her husband, the poet Christopher Woodman, handles correspondence in English.

What this concept fixes
