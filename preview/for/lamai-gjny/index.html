Classes

Each class has five courses. For each course you choose one of two dishes.

08.30 to 12.30Morning classFive courses, about four hours
  1. Chicken satay or glass noodle salad
  2. Massaman curry or roasted duck red curry
  3. Tom kha gai or tom yam goong
  4. Stir fried chicken with cashew nuts or sweet and sour fish
  5. Pumpkin in coconut milk or banana in coconut milk
13.30 to 17.30Afternoon classFive courses, about four hours
  1. Spring rolls or seafood salad
  2. Panang curry or green curry with chicken
  3. Tom kha gai or tom yam goong
  4. Fried shrimp with tamarind sauce or phad thai
  5. Pumpkin in coconut milk or banana in coconut milk
