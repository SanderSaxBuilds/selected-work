How a finish reads

Three numbers decide what a surface is made of.

Roughness

How wide the lamp's reflection spreads. Polish keeps it a sharp window, satin smears it into a glow, matte spreads it until only shading is left. The two smaller rings always run one and two steps rougher than the specimen, so you can read the scale at a glance.

Metal

Metals have no body colour of their own, only a tinted reflection. Turn metal down on brass and the gold leaves the highlights and moves into a painted base under a white sheen.

Clear coat

Enamel and lacquer carry a thin, glossy layer on top of their colour. It catches the room at grazing angles, which is why the edges of the red ring stay crisp even where its paint is dark.
