Original result, original time

A record is not a fresh check.

History describes the completed run. Opening it does not reread its archives, update verification dates or test recovery. Use Check every backup to inspect current saved copies.

Open current library entry selects the matching archive when its library record is still present. From there, verify files or review a recovery into a new folder. Recovery checks the saved archive again and refuses an existing destination.

Reports omit absolute workspace and storage paths. Labels and relative filenames can still identify your work. Review the JSON before sharing it. Hashes establish consistency, not ownership or a trusted origin.

Limits that matter.

  • Manual developer preview tested on Windows with Node.js 24.19.0 and the current browser. Linux and macOS remain untested.
  • Up to 500 files, 20 MiB per file and 50 MiB of source data per archive. Imports are limited to 52 MiB.
  • Unencrypted Archive Desk ZIP Store format only. Arbitrary compressed, encrypted and ZIP64 archives are unsupported.
  • Filename exclusions are not sensitive-content detection. File paths and contents are restored, not original timestamps, permissions or empty folders.
  • Scanning is not an atomic snapshot or an adversarial filesystem race guarantee. Stop other editors and keep a separate copy on another device.

Independent product work with synthetic data. This is not a production backup service or evidence of customer acceptance.
