Original SVG studio renders of the fictional Fieldnote Stillwater bottle.

Each file is named colour-size-view.svg. Colours are Moss, Glacier and Basalt, sizes are 500 and 750 ml, and the views are front, open (lid off and set down), detail (cap and steel lip) and sizes (both sizes side by side). They are drawn from one parametric model: a lit cylinder gradient, softbox and rim highlights that follow the shoulder, a powder coat grain, a polished steel lip, a knurled cap with a carry loop, contact and cast shadows and a faint floor reflection. No third-party product photographs are included.
