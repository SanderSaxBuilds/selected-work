Course outline

Each module has two short lessons and one practice exercise you can use on your next tour.

1Understand the prospect's priorities3 lessons, 35 min

Replace a broad opening with two focused questions, and find out what the prospect needs to understand before you explain anything.

  • Why the first two questions matter8 min
  • Replacing a broad opener12 min
  • Write two focused questions15 min
2Explain the options that matter3 lessons, 37 min

Compare two options using the priorities the prospect already stated, and keep a known fact apart from an assumption that still needs checking.

  • Linking options to stated priorities10 min
  • Facts and assumptions to confirm12 min
  • Compare two options out loud15 min
3Agree a useful follow-up3 lessons, 33 min

Summarize the open question, name the next action and who owns it, and agree when it will happen.

  • Summarizing the open question8 min
  • Naming the next action and its owner10 min
  • Rewrite a vague follow-up15 min

Questions

How long do I have access?

Twelve months from the day you enroll, on any device. You can repeat lessons as often as you like.

Do I need leasing experience?

No. New agents use it to build habits and experienced agents use the exercises as a refresher. The examples assume apartment tours, but the moments apply to any viewing.

Is there a version for teams?

Yes. The team pathway gives up to ten learners the course plus a discussion guide and review worksheets for three team sessions.
