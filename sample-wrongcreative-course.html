Who this concept is for

Teams practicing prospect conversations.

Actual duration, access period, prerequisites, price and learning outcomes are not supplied by the buyer.

Fictional module outline

Three moments with a practical next step.

01. Understand the prospect's priorities

Example activity: replace a broad opening with two focused questions. Identify what the prospect wants to understand before explaining options.

02. Explain the relevant options

Example activity: compare two options using the priorities already stated. Separate a known fact from an assumption that needs confirmation.

03. Agree a useful follow-up

Example activity: summarize the open question, name the next action and check who is responsible. Use only the buyer's approved process.
