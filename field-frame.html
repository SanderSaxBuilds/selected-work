    A-06General notes

    What people ask before the first visit.

    N1Is the price really fixed?

    Yes, for everything written in the scope. If you ask for a change during the build, we price it in writing first and only go ahead once you sign it.

    N2Do you handle drawings and permits?

    For additions and new homes we work with two local architects and submit the permit application for you. For renovations we can work from your existing drawings or measure up ourselves.

    N3Can we stay in the house during the work?

    Usually, yes. We plan the order of work so you keep a working kitchen or bathroom for as long as possible, and we tell you in advance about the days that will be noisy or without water.

    N4How soon can you start?

    Start dates are set when you accept the quote. Smaller kitchen and bathroom jobs often start within six to eight weeks, larger jobs after drawings and permits are in place.

    N5Which areas do you cover?

    Westbrook, Alder Falls, Fernbrook and the villages within about forty minutes of our yard on the river road.
