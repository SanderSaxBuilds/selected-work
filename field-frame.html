A few useful answers

Before you
get started.

Is Field & Frame a real contractor?

No. It is a fictional portfolio concept. No construction services are offered and no client projects are shown.

Does this page send my project details?

No. The brief builder previews entries in this page and creates a text download locally. There is no form service, account, analytics or message destination connected.

Are the project images completed work?

No. The exterior photograph is licensed reference photography. It does not show a Field & Frame project.

What would a real project need next?

A real contractor would confirm services, location, approved photography, content, enquiry destination, platform and hosting before publication.
