Work we take on

Four kinds of job, and the time each one usually spends on site.

Whole-home renovation

16 to 30 wk

Older houses rewired, replumbed and insulated, with walls moved so every room earns its place.

  • Structural openings and steel
  • Rewiring, plumbing, heating
  • Insulation and windows

Additions and extensions

12 to 20 wk

A family room, an office or a bigger kitchen that finally opens onto the garden.

  • Drawings and permit application
  • Foundations, frame and roof
  • Tie-in to the existing house

Kitchens and bathrooms

6 to 10 wk

Rooms planned around how you cook, clean and get ready, then fitted with finishes that last.

  • Layout and cabinetry plans
  • Tiling, worktops, fixtures
  • Ventilation and lighting

New homes

9 to 14 mo

Built from your architect's drawings or one of our two partner practices, on your plot and to a written specification.

  • Site preparation and groundwork
  • Timber or block construction
  • Full fit-out to handover

    How a job runs

    Five fixed points, and you always know which one you are at.

    1. Week 0

      Site visit

      We walk the house with you, measure up and listen to what is not working. The first visit has no charge.

    2. Within 10 working days

      Scope and fixed price

      A written scope, a fixed price and a start date. Nothing changes without your signature.

    3. Before the start

      Drawings and permits

      For additions and new homes we prepare drawings with a partner architect and submit the permit application.

    4. On site

      Build

      Your site lead runs the trades and the schedule, and sends the Friday note with photos and next decisions.

    5. Handover, then 12 months

      Walkthrough and return

      We walk every room, clear the snag list, then come back after a year to check how the house has settled.

    Questions

    What people ask before the first visit.

    Is the price really fixed?

    Yes, for everything written in the scope. If you ask for a change during the build, we price it in writing first and only go ahead once you sign it.

    Do you handle drawings and permits?

    For additions and new homes we work with two local architects and submit the permit application for you. For renovations we can work from your existing drawings or measure up ourselves.

    Can we stay in the house during the work?

    Usually, yes. We plan the order of work so you keep a working kitchen or bathroom for as long as possible, and we tell you in advance about the days that will be noisy or without water.

    How soon can you start?

    Start dates are set when you accept the quote. Smaller kitchen and bathroom jobs often start within six to eight weeks, larger jobs after drawings and permits are in place.

    Which areas do you cover?

    Westbrook, Alder Falls, Fernbrook and the villages within about forty minutes of our yard on the river road.
