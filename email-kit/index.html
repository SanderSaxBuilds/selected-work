What it handles

  • Line totals, the discount and the total worked out at build time from unit prices, then checked again by the tests
  • Delivery and payment details in two fluid hybrid columns that stack on phones without a media query
  • Ghost tables in Outlook conditional comments keep those columns side by side in Outlook
  • Thumbnails sized by width and height attributes, so Outlook on a high DPI screen keeps them at 56 pixels
  • Figures right aligned with tabular numbers
