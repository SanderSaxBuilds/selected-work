Claude Mail

A private mail helper for one person's own Gmail accounts.

What it is

Claude Mail is a small tool that runs on its owner's computer. It lets the owner search, read, draft and send email in Gmail accounts that the owner controls, with the help of an AI assistant working at the owner's direction.

Who can use it

Only its owner. Claude Mail is not a public service and is not offered to other people. If you reached a Google sign-in screen for Claude Mail by mistake, close it. Nothing happens unless you sign in.

Privacy

How the tool handles Gmail data is described in the privacy policy.

Claude Mail is a personal project by SanderSaxBuilds.
